# Acknowledgement of generative AI use

The rubric marks "acknowledgement of sources and permitted AI use" under criterion 6.
Complete this honestly and include it in your submission. Check your unit outline for
the exact declaration format your institution requires — it may differ from this
template, and where it does, theirs wins.

## Why this matters

An inaccurate or missing declaration is an academic integrity matter, and it is
separate from the question of whether AI use was permitted. Declaring use that was
permitted costs you nothing. Failing to declare it is the risk.

## Template

> ### Use of generative AI in this assessment
>
> **Tool used:** *(name and version, e.g. Claude Opus 4.5, Anthropic)*
> **Dates of use:** *(e.g. 2–6 September 2026)*
>
> **What it was used for:**
> - *(e.g. Generating the initial React component scaffold and CSS design token layer)*
> - *(e.g. Drafting the structure and first pass of this reflection)*
> - *(e.g. Reviewing form validation logic for accessibility gaps)*
>
> **What the team did:**
> - *(e.g. Selected the problem domain and defined all functional requirements)*
> - *(e.g. Reviewed, tested, corrected and extended all generated code before committing)*
> - *(e.g. Wrote all individual reflections and the contribution statement)*
> - *(e.g. Deployed, tested and verified the application against the brief)*
>
> **Verification:** All generated code was read, run and tested by the team before it
> was committed. *(Describe what you actually checked — that is the point of this
> paragraph.)*
>
> Signed: *(each member's name and student ID)*

## Two things to get right

1. **Be specific.** "AI was used for assistance" is not a declaration. Name what it
   touched.
2. **Do not overstate the team's contribution or understate the tool's.** The commit
   history is visible to your marker, and so is the gap between what a team can explain
   in a demonstration and what it submitted.

## Referencing AI in APA 7th

Reference the tool where you cite its output directly:

> Anthropic. (2026). *Claude* (Sept 2026 version) [Large language model].
> https://claude.ai

Check the APA style guidance your unit points to; conventions for AI tools are still
settling and your institution may have its own rule.
